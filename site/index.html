How we work

  1. A first conversation

    Tell us about the occasion, the guests and the feeling you imagine. Your enquiry basket can carry the pieces that inspired you.

  2. Concept & moodboard

    We translate the brief into a visual direction: palette, florals, table design and atmosphere, with a tailored proposal.

  3. Curation & detail

    Décor, rentals and flowers are confirmed for your date, along with timings, quantities and the plan for the day.

  4. Installation

    Our team sets the scene before guests arrive and clears it afterwards, so you can simply be present.
